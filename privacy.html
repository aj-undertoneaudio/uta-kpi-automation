Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

About This Application

UTA KPI Automation is a private reporting application used to collect authorized YouTube channel analytics and organize performance metrics in Google Sheets.

Information Accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the application accesses YouTube channel identification information and analytics, including views, subscribers gained, and average view duration.

How Information Is Used

Information is used exclusively to generate and maintain internal performance reports. The application does not upload videos, modify YouTube channel content, or sell personal information.

Storage and Sharing

Reporting data is stored in access-controlled Google Sheets. OAuth authorization tokens are maintained within the application's Google Apps Script environment. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Data Retention and Deletion

Reporting data is retained for internal historical analysis until it is deleted by the application operator. Authorized users may request deletion of their associated reporting data.

Revoking Access

Users may revoke the application's Google account access through their Google Account settings at https://myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the application operator using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
